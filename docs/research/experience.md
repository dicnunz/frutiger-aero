# Why this loop

Reviewed 8 October 2026. These are design constraints drawn from primary work, followed by our adaptation. Download counts and active maintenance establish substantial adoption; they do not measure retention or prove universal superiority.

| [Kevin Gabeci, AI game-art criticism, 17 July 2026](https://shipagame.dev/blog/ai-game-art), [shipping critique, 20 July 2026](https://shipagame.dev/blog/how-to-make-a-game-with-ai) | Inconsistent tiling, unreadable interfaces and decoration over an untested loop are practical failure modes. | Keep the existing texture palette, add geometric port indicators, retain native Minecraft controls, and verify in-engine behavior and repeat visits. A generator stress test establishes correctness, not long-term enjoyment. |

The implementation is a focused exploration-and-building mod. It does not install Create or Vault Hunters or claim their content volume. Endless means new seed-driven expeditions and renewable building resources without a final mandatory run; the authored room roles and plant catalog are finite. Longer-term appeal still needs player feedback.

# Trailer constraints

[Derek Lieu's 2025 trailer critique](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2025/12/15/my-favorite-game-trailers-of-2025), [gameplay ordering](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2023/7/16/how-to-decide-what-to-show-in-a-game-trailer-and-in-what-order-to-put-it), and [editing rhythm](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2018/1/6/what-im-thinking-when-i-edit-a-trailer) inform a short gameplay arc: a flow's cause and effect, movement into another space, discovery, return, construction and another departure. The user's presentation constraints remove HUD, menus, titles and CTA text. Recorded movement uses Minecraft's input, interpolation and collision; turns remain restrained. Cuts give actions room to read and preserve tactile game sounds under music.
